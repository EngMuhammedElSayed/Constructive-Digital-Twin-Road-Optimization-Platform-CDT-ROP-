# Project Charter

---

# Project Name

Constructive Digital Twin Road Optimization Platform (CDT-ROP)

---

# Vision

Develop a professional engineering platform capable of optimizing highway geometric design using Constructive Digital Twin technologies and multi-objective optimization while integrating seamlessly with Autodesk Civil 3D.
---

# Mission

Provide highway engineers with an intelligent decision-support platform that evaluates multiple design alternatives and recommends optimal solutions based on engineering standards, safety, and cost.
---

# Research Problem

Traditional highway design processes rely heavily on manual engineering judgment and iterative design modifications, making it difficult to evaluate numerous design alternatives while simultaneously satisfying engineering constraints, economic objectives, and roadway safety requirements.
---

# Project Objectives

- Develop a Constructive Digital Twin framework.
- Integrate BIM and optimization.
- Implement NSGA-II.
- Minimize construction cost.
- Maximize roadway safety.
- Support Civil 3D.
- Generate engineering reports.
---

# Scope

The platform focuses on highway geometric design optimization for urban roads using BIM, Digital Twin concepts, and multi-objective optimization.

Current case study:

26th of July Corridor

Hyper One → Al-Hosary Square

6th October City

Egypt
---

# Out of Scope

The current research does not include:

- Pavement structural design
- Drainage network design
- Utility relocation
- Structural bridge design
- Construction scheduling
- Machine learning prediction models
